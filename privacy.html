SG Toto Chartist — Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

This privacy policy applies to the SG Toto Chartist mobile application (shown as “Toto Chartist” inside the app), together with any related services operated by Toto Chartist (collectively, the “Application”). Toto Chartist is hereby referred to as the “Service Provider”.

Nature of the Application

SG Toto Chartist is a lifestyle and hobby application for analysing publicly published historical lottery draw results. It performs statistical analysis — frequency counts, gap and pattern statistics, and illustrative number combinations — for information and entertainment purposes only.

It is a statistics, charting and reference tool. It is not a gambling application.

No affiliation with any lottery operator

The Application is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to Singapore Pools, or any other lottery operator, gaming operator, or government body.

“Toto” refers to the lottery game whose publicly published results the Application analyses. All product names, brands and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

No gambling functionality

The Application does not offer gambling and cannot be used to gamble. Specifically, it does not:

  • sell, reserve, validate or redeem lottery tickets;
  • accept, place or settle bets, wagers or stakes;
  • enable entry into any lottery, draw or game of chance;
  • handle money, deposits, withdrawals or winnings;
  • provide real-money gaming of any kind.

The Application contains no in-app currency, no wagering and no prizes. Purchases in the Application are subscriptions that unlock analysis features only.

No prediction or guaranteed outcome

Lottery draws are random. Every combination has the same chance in every draw, and past results do not influence future results.

The charts, statistics and generated number combinations in the Application are descriptive summaries of historical data. They cannot predict future results and they do not improve your chances of winning. No outcome is guaranteed or implied, and nothing in the Application constitutes betting, financial or professional advice.

Responsible play (18+)

The Application is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Gambling should be a leisure activity — never a way to make money or to recover losses.

If you or someone you know is struggling with gambling, support is available from the National Council on Problem Gambling (Singapore) at ncpg.org.sg, or from Gambling Therapy worldwide at gamblingtherapy.org. If gambling stops being fun, stop.

Information Collection and Use

The Application does not require an account or registration, and the Service Provider does not operate a server that stores your personal data.

The Service Provider does not collect personal information from you automatically. Information reaches the Service Provider only if you choose to provide it:

  • an email address you optionally enter in the subscription details screen (see Optional Email Address for Subscription Support below);
  • your email address and the contents of your message if you contact support.

Information provided this way is used only to respond to you, to support your subscription, and to improve the Application.

Optional Email Address for Subscription Support

You may optionally provide an email address in the Application’s subscription details screen. It is not required; the Application works fully without it.

If you provide one, that email address is sent to RevenueCat (see Third Party Access below) and attached to your subscription profile, so that:

  • your subscription can be linked to you when you contact support;
  • you can be identified when restoring a purchase or resolving a billing query.

The email address is used only for subscription support and receipts. It is not used for marketing, it is not sold or shared with anyone else, and it is not used to track you across other applications or websites.

You can change or remove the email address at any time in the same screen, or by contacting the Service Provider at Toto.chartist@gmail.com.

No Cookies, No Tracking, No Advertising

The Application does not use cookies, pixels, web beacons or similar tracking technologies. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDK. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not track the screens or pages you view, and does not ask for permission to track you across other applications or websites.

Information Stored on Your Device

The Application stores the following data locally on your device only. It is not transmitted to the Service Provider:

  • your preferences and settings (for example, generator settings, favourites and theme);
  • a cached copy of historical draw data, so charts load quickly and work offline.

This data remains on your device until you uninstall the Application.

Historical Data and Third-Party Sources

The Application retrieves publicly published historical draw results from public sources, such as the Singapore Pools website and public Google Sheets CSV exports. As with any internet request, the operator of the source site may see the IP address of your device in the ordinary course of serving that request. The Service Provider does not send your personal data to these sources, and does not receive or retain any data about you from them.

Third Party Access

The Service Provider does not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or any other purpose.

The Application uses a limited number of third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • RevenueCat — used to validate subscription status and, where you provide one, to hold the email address attached to your subscription
  • Google Play Services — used for in-app purchases on Android
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — used for in-app purchases and subscription management on iOS

The Service Provider may disclose information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when it believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • with trusted service providers who work on its behalf, who have no independent use of the information disclosed to them, and who have agreed to adhere to the rules set out in this privacy statement.

In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Premium features are sold as auto-renewable subscriptions. On iOS, payment is processed by Apple; on Android, by Google Play. The Service Provider does not receive your name, card details or billing address.

RevenueCat receives an anonymised app user identifier and the store’s purchase receipt in order to confirm whether your subscription is active. It does not receive your name or your payment card details. If you optionally provide an email address as described above, that email address is attached to your subscription profile.

You can cancel a subscription at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the paid period.

International Data Transfers

The Service Provider does not operate servers. However, the third-party services listed above (RevenueCat, Apple and Google) may process data in countries outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, those providers use appropriate mechanisms, which may include:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions or other legally recognised transfer mechanisms
  • Your consent, where required and legally permitted

Data protection laws in other countries may differ from those in your jurisdiction. Where required by law, appropriate safeguards will be applied and any required consent obtained.

Your Rights

To exercise these rights, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, contact the Service Provider at Toto.chartist@gmail.com.

Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights.

The Service Provider does not sell or share personal information. To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights, contact the Service Provider at Toto.chartist@gmail.com.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop the Application from storing data on your device by uninstalling it. Uninstalling removes the data stored locally by the Application. You can also remove the optional subscription email address in the subscription details screen.

To request deletion of personal data the Service Provider holds (for example, support emails), to withdraw consent, or to exercise any of your rights, contact the Service Provider at Toto.chartist@gmail.com.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider retains personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Data stored locally by the Application: retained on your device until you uninstall the Application.
  • Optional subscription email address: retained by RevenueCat while your subscription profile exists, until you remove it in the Application or request deletion.
  • Support correspondence: retained while your enquiry is open and for as long as reasonably necessary afterwards, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Purchase and subscription records: held and retained by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own privacy policies and retention periods.
  • Data required for legal compliance: retained as long as required by applicable law.

You may request deletion of personal data, subject to any legal obligation to retain it, by contacting the Service Provider at Toto.chartist@gmail.com.

Data Deletion

You can remove all data stored locally by the Application by uninstalling it. Because the Service Provider does not operate a user account system or a server storing your personal data, there is no account to delete.

You can remove the optional subscription email address yourself in the Application’s subscription details screen. To have it deleted from RevenueCat, or to request deletion of support correspondence, contact the Service Provider at Toto.chartist@gmail.com. Requests will be processed within the timeframes required by applicable law. Upon verification of your identity, the Service Provider will delete that personal data, except where retention is required for legal compliance.

Subscription records held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat must be managed through those providers’ own privacy and account tools.
